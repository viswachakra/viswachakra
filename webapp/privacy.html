Privacy Policy

Viswachakra Claims (iOS and Android app, and the web dashboard at viswachakra.vercel.app) · Effective 7 October 2026

Viswachakra Claims is an internal claims-tracking tool built for Viswa Chakra Orthopaedic Hospital, Machilipatnam, Andhra Pradesh, to follow the hospital's Dr. YSR Aarogyasri / NTR Vaidya Seva insurance claims from admission to payment. It is operated by VVIS Technologies (OPC) Private Limited on behalf of the hospital. The app is not offered to the general public: only hospital staff with an account issued by the hospital can sign in.

What the app shows

The app displays claim records that the hospital already holds in the government Aarogyasri portal. For each case this includes:

  • Patient name, Aarogyasri card number, district, mandal and village, and the contact number recorded in the claim
  • Admission and discharge dates, procedure name, and claim status history
  • Claimed, approved and paid amounts, and the dates of each step

These records are copied from the hospital's own portal account into a database controlled by the hospital (hosted on Supabase, with servers located outside India) and read by the app. The app does not create patient records, and it does not collect any information from patients directly.

What we collect from you, the user

DataWhy
Your work email address and passwordTo sign you in. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth and are never visible to us.

A short-lived session token on the deviceSo you stay signed in.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or advertising identifiers. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. It does not track you across other apps or websites.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows alerts on your device when a claim is paid, queried or stalls. These are generated on the device from the claim data; nothing about your notifications is sent to any server.

Data kept on the device

The app keeps a copy of the claim list on the device so it opens quickly and can raise alerts. This copy is removed when you sign out or delete the app.

Who can see the data

  • Hospital staff who have been given an account, according to their role.
  • VVIS Technologies, as the technical operator, for maintenance and support only.
  • Supabase, as the database host, under its own data-processing terms.

We do not sell, rent or share claim or user data with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

Retention

Claim records are retained for as long as the hospital needs them for its accounts and audits. User accounts are deleted when the hospital asks us to remove a staff member.

Security

All traffic between the app and the database is encrypted (HTTPS). Access requires a password and is restricted by role. Database access from the app is read-only.

Your rights

Hospital staff can ask for their account to be corrected or deleted by writing to the address below. Patients whose records appear in the app should contact the hospital directly; the hospital is the data fiduciary for patient records under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Children

The app is for hospital staff and is not directed at children under 18.
